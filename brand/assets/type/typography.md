Rules
- **GE Dinar One** is used for headings and short Arabic phrases only. It has one weight, no Latin letters,
  and it draws the Western digits 0–9 with Arabic-Indic shapes, so **Western numerals, amounts, dates and
  Latin text are always set in IBM Plex Sans**, even inside headings.
- Never track (letter-space) Arabic. No kashida for justification. Line height 1.5–1.7 for text.
- Minimum sizes: 11 px on screen, 7 pt in print; GE Dinar One no smaller than 12 px.
- Amounts: `1,250.50 د.ل` (Western digits, currency after the number). LYD has 3 minor units (ISO 4217);
  the number of decimals shown is pending a finance decision.
- The brand name is the drawn logotype; never retype «سداد ليبيا» as the logo in any font.
